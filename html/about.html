Planetary Characteristics

A comprehensive astrophysical breakdown of revolution periods, axial tilt, and environmental extremes across the solar system.

T

Orbital Revolution (Year)

The time required for a celestial body to complete one full revolution around the solar barycenter, determining its seasonal and calendar year.

ω

Axial Rotation (Day)

The time taken for a planet to complete 360 degrees of rotation on its polar axis. Venus and Uranus exhibit retrograde spin.

AU

Semi-Major Axis (Distance)

Mean distance from the Sun measured in Astronomical Units (1 AU ≈ 149.6M km). Governs solar irradiance and thermal balance.
