About Planetary Ages

Understanding how time works differently across our solar system

What Makes Each Planet Unique?

Each planet in our solar system has a different orbital period around the Sun and rotational period on its axis. This means that a "year" and a "day" on each planet is vastly different from what we experience on Earth.

🌍

Orbital Period

The time it takes a planet to complete one full orbit around the Sun. This determines the length of a year on that planet.

🌀

Rotational Period

The time it takes a planet to complete one full rotation on its axis. This determines the length of a day on that planet.

📏

Distance from Sun

The farther a planet is from the Sun, the longer its orbital period and the older you would be in Earth years.
